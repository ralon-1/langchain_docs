The roar of the crowd, a thunderous cheer,
The lush green field, so crisp and clear.
The stumps stand tall, in orderly rows,
Awaiting the bowler's mighty throws.

The bat, a willow blade, so light, so true,
In the batsman's hands, it's a weapon anew.
The crease is marked, the field is set,
The umpire calls, "Play, and let it be fair and met."

The bowler runs, with grace and might,
The ball leaves hand, a swift, white flight.
The batsman stands, with eyes so keen,
To meet the ball, to score and glean.

The bat connects, a crack resounds,
The ball soars high, through the air it bounds.
The fielders dash, with all their might,
But the ball clears the ropes, a six in sight.

The spinner turns, the ball deceives,
The batsman's eyes, it often thieves.
A flick, a spin, a wily art,
The batsman's stumps, may soon depart.

The crowd erupts, in joy or woe,
For every run, for every blow.
The game goes on, through sun and rain,
A dance of skill, a test of brain.

The final ball, the last over's end,
The scores are tallied, the winner's penned.
The handshakes given, the game is done,
But in our hearts, the love has won.
